02 / OUR METHODOLOGY

Terms, not guesswork.

These are editorial assessments of linked official documentation and attributed maintainer assessments, checked on the date shown. We haven’t tested accounts or negotiated contracts. Terms change; the receipts matter more than the badge.

Something changed? Suggest a correction ↗

We distinguish access to the protocol from ordinary product subscriptions, AI inference, infrastructure, API limits and resource usage. A feature entitlement alone doesn’t establish a protocol surcharge. Unknown doesn’t mean free. Announced future fees aren’t current charges.

Included
Explicit included or no-extra-charge MCP access. Product fees can still apply.
Limited free
A free MCP quota or partial access, with paid expansion.
Plan gated
A paid plan or seat is a prerequisite for the reviewed server.
Metered
MCP-specific fees, credits or MCP activity that creates a billable seat.
Unclear
Official information doesn’t establish current pricing or availability.

Only explicitly supported included-access records join the honor roll. Mixed offerings, preview status and delivery model stay visible in each record.
