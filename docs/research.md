# Research notes

Initial coverage: 37 official vendor MCP offerings, checked November 10, 2026. The canonical source URLs, claim paraphrases, checked dates, deployment scope and caveats are in `data/vendors.json`. This is an editorial comparison of access terms, not a complete directory or a billing audit.

Researchers reviewed official documentation, pricing pages, vendor repositories, release notes and seller-identified marketplace listings. Specific current server and pricing guidance takes precedence over older announcements or broad feature labels when the record explains the conflict. Search snippets alone do not establish a current price. Dollar amounts are retained only where the research established currency, unit and billing cadence; no percentage uplift is inferred.

Status describes the reviewed offering: `included` needs explicit included/all-plan or no-extra-charge evidence; `limited` covers MCP quotas or partial paid-tool access; `gated` covers a paid prerequisite; `metered` covers MCP-specific charges, credits, tasks or billable seats; any unconfirmed price or billing unit remains explicit; `unclear` preserves gaps in published terms. Ordinary API limits, resource usage, AI processing and client subscriptions are described separately. Honor-roll eligibility additionally requires explicit included-access evidence. It does not promise every tool is free, unlimited or available to every user.

Important conflicts and transitions:

- GitLab's specific server guide and 19.2 release announcement establish Free access, despite the umbrella MCP page retaining a paid-tier label.
- Figma's current live rate-limits page gives a 20-call monthly allowance for affected seats. Older official indexed material gave six; do not mix the versions. Its announced future usage pricing concerns canvas writing during beta, not all MCP calls.
- Box's current pricing guide says all plans; its older GA announcement says Business and above. Free API calls additionally require a published integration and personal OAuth; other workflows incur API charges.
- ClickUp is migrating plans. New Max and legacy Everything AI have different packaging; new and existing Enterprise quotas also differ. Indexed legacy prices are excluded.
- Coda became Superhuman Docs. The current Docs MCP permits Editor writes under a limited allowance; legacy Coda Editor restrictions and retained customer pricing are separate.
- monday.com's dedicated API documentation gives a Free daily limit of 200, conflicting with a general AI page's 1,000. The record retains that conflict.
- Asana's explicit free Marketplace offering and free-account integration availability support its scoped included classification. A universal direct V2 fee policy remains unverified. Netlify's explicit connection-cost evidence is similarly scoped to its documented coding-agent workflow.

Announced changes remain future-dated as of the checked date: Atlassian extra-usage billing on December 3, 2026; Zapier billing exceptions on December 1, 2026; and Salesforce HPI metering with a multiplier still TBA and a promised 30-day notice. That HPI announcement does not establish the tariff for the charged hosted MCP offering clarified below. Figma canvas-writing beta pricing has no asserted start date. Sentry's pricing evidence mentions a December 8 Seer credit change, distinct from MCP access. Stripe's November 30 API-key migration deadline concerns authentication, not a new MCP fee.

No customer accounts, paid subscriptions, invoices or live billing behavior were tested. Tool availability, negotiated pricing, regional pricing and admin controls can differ. `unclear` means the reviewed evidence does not settle the question; missing pricing is not proof of free access. The JSON validator checks metadata structure and consistency, not the truth, freshness or completeness of source claims. The initial assembly did not independently reopen every source URL; the focused follow-up below reopened its decisive primary sources.

There is no automated ongoing source monitoring. Checked dates age, announced changes may take effect, and contributions need a fresh primary source plus the affected scope and checked date. Source excerpts are paraphrased evidence notes; the code license does not grant rights to third-party source material, names or marks.

## Cloudflare correction

On November 10, 2026 the project maintainer clarified that Cloudflare does not charge for MCP and that a Free-tier plan is sufficient. This direct confirmation supersedes the earlier unresolved classification and qualifies the hosted API MCP entry for the honor roll.

The linked Cloudflare pages establish setup and permissions; the pricing correction is attributed to the maintainer, not presented as an explicit vendor-published fee statement. No authenticated account or invoice test was performed. Ordinary charges for underlying Cloudflare products remain separate from MCP access.

## Salesforce and Slack corrections

On November 10, 2026 the project maintainer confirmed that Salesforce charges for hosted MCP and that Slack MCP is free. Salesforce moves out of `unclear` into the charged category (`metered`); its public price and billing unit remain unconfirmed. No per-call rate, amount or minimum commitment is inferred. Slack joins the included-access honor roll; this does not assert every tool is available on every ordinary Slack plan.

These fee classifications are attributed to the maintainer. Official documentation establishes technical access, billing warnings and scoped exceptions; it is not represented as a published price or blanket fee waiver. Public HPI guidance leaves a TBA multiplier; its relationship to the confirmed charge remains unconfirmed. Developer Edition and Data 360 inclusion remain visible. No accounts, invoices or private contracts were tested.

## Datadog correction — November 11, 2026

The maintainer assessed Datadog MCP as included for existing customers, without a separate MCP upsell. Official GA guidance directs existing customers to connect, and the current setup guide lists account permissions and administrator enablement. Requiring an ordinary Datadog customer account is not, by itself, an MCP tax. Datadog therefore joins the included-access honor roll.

A November 11 recheck also found that the current server guide explicitly states there is no monthly tool-call limit. This supersedes the 100,000-call figure in our November 10 record; the exact timing of the documentation change was not established. The 50-calls-per-10-seconds burst protection remains adjustable and subject to change. No unlimited throughput or free underlying product usage is implied.

The no-upsell assessment is attributed to the maintainer and the documented customer-access path, not presented as a universal vendor-published fee waiver. No authenticated account, invoice or private contract was tested.
